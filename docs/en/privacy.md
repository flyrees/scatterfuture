# Scatter Future Privacy Policy

---

- Last updated: November 25, 2026
- Effective date: November 25, 2026

---

## Introduction

Scatter Future (the "App") takes your personal information seriously. We have established this policy in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, and other applicable laws and regulations to explain how we collect, use, store, and protect your information. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

## 1. Information We Collect and Use

1. **Information you enter**: income and expense records (amount, category, date, note), asset information (name, category, purchase price, current price, quantity, note), account book names, nickname, and avatar. This information is stored only in the App's sandbox on your device. We do not upload it to our servers, nor do we read or use it for other purposes.
2. **Membership purchase information**: the annual Pro membership (CNY 28 per year) is purchased through the HUAWEI AppGallery in-app purchase service. We do not collect or store your bank card or other payment information; we only store the membership expiration date locally to unlock membership features.
3. **App settings**: theme, income and expense colors, reminder toggles, app lock, first-use date, and similar settings are stored locally only.
4. **Photo library and images**: when you change your avatar or save a share image, the App accesses only the image you select through the system photo picker or system save control. We do not read other content in your photo library and do not upload images.
5. We do not collect your location, contacts, call history, messages, clipboard content, or device identifiers, and the App contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## 2. System Permissions

1. **Notification permission**: used to remind you to record bills at 21:00 each day after you enable "Daily Reminder". You can turn it off at any time in the App or system settings.
2. **Biometric permission**: used only for local identity verification when you enable the passcode lock and choose face or fingerprint unlock. Biometric information is handled by the system security module and cannot be accessed by the App.
3. **Network permission**: used only to open the AppGallery review page, share links, load the web versions of these terms and this policy, and (if enabled) membership purchase and cloud sync. When loading the legal pages, only text is loaded; your bookkeeping information is never uploaded.
Apart from the permissions above, the App does not request other sensitive permissions. Denying authorization will not affect core features such as bookkeeping (except the corresponding features).

## 3. Storage and Protection

1. Storage location: your data is stored locally on your device by default and is not transferred outside the country.
2. Retention: data is kept while you use the App; it is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear data under "Data Management".
3. Security: we use the system app sandbox, data encryption, and app lock to protect your data. Please understand that no technical measure can guarantee absolute security; keep your device safe.

## 4. Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure

1. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party.
2. When you actively use system share, Tap-to-Share (knock share), or the AppGallery review feature, the content is processed by the target app or system service you choose; please follow their privacy policies.
3. If you enable HUAWEI Cloud "Cloud Backup", your data will be encrypted and stored by HUAWEI Cloud under the system backup mechanism, and we cannot access it.
4. We will not publicly disclose your information except as required by laws and regulations or by judicial authorities.

## 5. Your Rights

1. You can view, modify, or delete your bookkeeping and asset data in the App at any time.
2. You can export or clear data through "Settings → Data Management".
3. You can uninstall the App to delete all local data.
4. If you have questions or complaints about how your personal information is processed, or need to exercise your rights, please contact us using the details below. We will reply as soon as possible.

## 6. Protection of Minors

The App is primarily intended for adults. If you are under 14 years old, please read this policy with your guardian and use the App only with your guardian's consent.

## 7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be prominently announced in the App, and material changes will require your consent again. We will not reduce your rights under this policy without your explicit consent.
